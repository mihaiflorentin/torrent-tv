Visual direction for Torrent TV

Three dark directions with a saffron accent and a blurred poster backdrop, as decided for ticket #29. Each frame shows the same moment: Home, the focused title, and a source row. Posters are fictional gradients. Each direction was checked against the hard limits: original look, readable at 3 m, obvious focus, no looping animation, Chromium 53 and 63 CSS.

A. Velvet Booth

Aubergine-black ink with saffron: orange against violet is a complement, so the accent reads as projector light in a dark room and not as a gold-on-grey streaming default. Bricolage Grotesque gives titles an opinionated voice at weight 700 to 800 and stays plain at 400. Signature: the backdrop follows focus, so the focused poster becomes the blurred room light while its neighbours dim. Source facts sit in a spec strip (resolution, codec, size, seeders). Chosen: it is the only one of the three that is distinct on all of palette, type and signature, and the signature costs nothing at runtime beyond one opacity crossfade.

B. Lamplit Study

Warm umber ink, cream text, a serif display face and an underline-style focus. Quiet and literary, with a flat editorial layout. Rejected: warm brown with gold and a serif is one of the common defaults for "premium dark", it sits close to existing gold-on-brown media apps, and a serif display face is weakest at 3 m on a low-density TV panel.

C. Graphite Gate

Cool graphite, a single saffron signal reserved for focus and selection, monospace for source facts to push the "source quality at the decision point" principle. Rejected: near-black with one bright accent is the other common default, and a monospace data face repeats the tracker-table look the product set out to avoid. Its one good idea, technical facts set apart from titles, is kept in A as the spec strip.
