# #54 TV six-hub rail, Library and Discover tabs, less screen chrome: screenshot review

What changed: the TV rail holds six hubs (Home, Search, Library, Discover, Downloads, Settings) instead of fifteen entries in four groups; Library and Discover draw a tab strip of their routes on the heading's line; Background jobs and Other projects are reached from Settings; the header Refresh button and the "Server connected" line are gone from every screen; the open rail shows the focused hub's hint, a count on Downloads and the connection at its foot; a banner appears only while the connection is lost or a tracker needs setting up; Search, Browse and the single-shelf Library tabs no longer repeat their name as a rail heading. Routes, the navigation snapshot and landing are unchanged (`clients/tv/src/hubs.ts` is presentation over them).

## How the captures were made

With the capture script, `node tools/tv_perf/screens.mjs tv` (headless Chromium at 1920x1080, Tizen 5 user agent, lean graphics, the synthetic household of `tools/tv_perf/fixture.mjs`, all 27 steps; the journey now walks hubs and tabs, see `ROUTE_PLACES` in `tools/tv_perf/journey.mjs`). "Before" is the integration tip 032630e (#52, #65, #53, #63, #66), built and captured with the same script; "after" is this branch. The boards were rendered from the mockup canvas (Navigation open, Home) at 1920x1080.

Committed here, downscaled to 720x405 so each stays under 300 KB: `54/{before,after}-rail-open.png`, `54/{before,after}-library-continue.png`, `54/{before,after}-browse.png`, `54/{before,after}-jobs.png`. The board renders could not be committed as reference images: rendered outside the canvas their template values (font, accent) stay unfilled, so they show a serif fallback. The full-size PNGs (360 to 950 KB) stay out of git; `make screens-tv` regenerates them.

Point by point:

- **Layout and spacing:** the content's left edge stays 168 px. Screens gain the 96 px header row back for content where it only held chrome (Home, Search start at the safe area). Tabs sit on the heading's line, 48 px after it, 16 px apart. The rail items keep one position compact and open.
- **Type:** rail labels 32 px semibold (bold for the current hub), hints 26 px medium (the floor), brand 34 px black; screen headings 56 px black with heading tracking.
- **Colour and accent:** saffron only on the Downloads count and the selected tab's bar. The hint is `--tt-text-faint` on the white focused pill; the connection dot is `--tt-ok` (and `--tt-warn` with "Reconnecting…"); the banner is a warn bar on `--tt-fill`.
- **Focus:** a focused rail item or tab is the white pill inside the white ring, like every control; tabs and rail rows never change their outer geometry on focus except the focused rail item growing for its hint (inside the rail's own layout containment).
- **Primary vs secondary:** the rail and tabs are navigation, not actions; Refresh leaves the screens (Reload library is in Settings › Server and is the next action of the empty catalog state).

## Deviations, with reasons

- **Rail items keep one position compact and open.** The board's open rail starts the items higher (y 228) than the compact strip (y 336). Moving them on open would re-lay out the rail on every open and break the compositor-only slide (#49), so both use one position (y 296).
- **The focused hub grows for its hint** (76 px to about 110 px, the hint wrapping to a second line for Library, as on the board): a layout change inside the rail only.
- **Rail item width 448 px** (board 440 px) so "Continue · My List · Watched · Categories" breaks after "Watched" rather than mid-list.
- **The compact strip is the panel's own fade** (the panel slides to show its last 120 px) instead of a separate gradient element; it reads as the board's soft strip.
- **Library has a fifth tab, "Dashboard"** (the board's hint lists Continue · My List · Watched · Categories). It keeps the `library` route (the stacked household rails) reachable: without a tab of its own the Library hub would open on Continue watching and the dashboard would have no way in. It is the hub's first tab, so a first visit to Library lands there.
- **An empty tab lands focus on the selected tab.** A Library or Discover tab with nothing in it (an empty My List, no titles yet) has no first card, so landing focus (`landing.ts`) takes the tab strip's selected tab (`data-landing="chrome"`) rather than the page body or the rail; Down then stays and Left opens the rail, as on any tab.
- **No board for the tab strip.** Tabs reuse the chips' pill and selected-marker vocabulary from #53; the board text "the selected-tab marker" is that saffron bar.
- **Home hero and cards are unchanged** (still the boxed hero): that is #55.
