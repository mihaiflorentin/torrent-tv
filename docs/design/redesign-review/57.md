# #57 TV series page in the new look: screenshot review

What changed: the series page loses its translucent card, its "Seasons" heading and its "Episodes · Season 1" heading; the header is the movie page's (#56) made compact as on the board (80 px title, 76 px actions) and leaves the overview to Info; the season tabs are the board's 64 px pills; the episode rows are the board's 176 px rows (256x144 still with the progress bar on it, quiet code and name, facts line with the version count last, two-line synopsis, one library-state word, the chevron); the list keeps the focused row in its middle; the Versions view sits on the page edge and opens with the season pills at the safe edge; season packs are rows on the quiet fill. A deeper bottom scrim on series pages replaces the card and its text halo.

## How the captures were made

With the #65 capture script, `node tools/tv_perf/screens.mjs tv --only series-title,series-episodes,series-versions` (headless Chromium at 1920x1080, Tizen 5 user agent, lean graphics, the synthetic household of `tools/tv_perf/fixture.mjs`). "Before" is the integration tip 89761fd (#52, #65, #53, #63, #66, #61, #56, #54), "after" is this branch. The fixture now gives the series the household is part-way through its episode states (earlier episodes watched, the resumed one in progress at the saved position, the next downloaded), so the "after" set shows the board's three states; the "before" set ran on the old fixture, whose episodes had no state. Committed here, half size and under 110 KB each: `57/{before,after}-series-title.png` (landing on Resume), `57/{before,after}-series-episodes.png` (cursor on the selected episode), `57/{before,after}-series-versions.png` (OK on it). The full-screen PNGs stay out of git; `make screens-tv` regenerates them.

## Deviations and their reasons

- **Selected season is not white.** The board draws it white; white marks the cursor (#53's focus model: "white is reserved for focus"), so the selected season keeps the selected-tab treatment (brighter fill, bold white word, saffron bar). A white selected pill would read as focus from the sofa.
- **Back, the facts line and the plays line stay** above the actions (the board has none of them): Back is row 0 of the declared map and story 39; the facts line and the plays line are the movie page's header (#56), which the ticket asks the series page to use. They cost about 140 px, so the first screen holds two and a half rows, not three.
- **No overview on the series page.** The board has none, and with it the list would show under two rows. The overview is in Info, one press right of Resume.
- **The synopsis is two lines**, as the ticket and DESIGN.md ask; the board's one line was a mockup simplification. Line heights are tightened (1.15 and 1.35) so name, facts and two synopsis lines fit the 144 px still and every row stays 176 px.
- **The facts line keeps the rating** ("6.5 / 10") from the season info; the board's line omits it.
- **One state word** stands in for the old download and watch badge pair: a failed download ("Needs attention") wins, then progress, Watched, the download state. A watched episode that is also on disk says "Watched" only.
- **Choose version** stays in the action row (#56); the board's series row omits it.

## D-pad (maps `series-title`, `series-versions`, `series-more`, `series-info`)

Unchanged: every stop, row, column and direction is as #46, #47 and #56 left them, so no map changed. What moves is the scroll: the list centres the focused row, a shown list centres its selected episode, opening Versions puts the pills at the safe edge and closing it returns the page to the top. `node tools/tv_perf/dpad.mjs --only movie-title,movie-info,movie-more,series-title,series-more,series-versions,series-info,info-seasons,info-season` passes (9 maps) with the new fixture states (the selected episode is now the resumed E04, the last of season 1).

## Performance

`node tools/tv_perf/run.mjs --lean` passes: worst D-pad move 46 ms (budget 50), worst route change 134.8 ms (budget 150); the harness does not visit title pages. A throwaway probe on the series page under the same 6x throttle and lean user agent measured episode moves at 4.7 to 8.1 ms average and 12.3 ms worst (held Up), Up to the season pill 5.3 ms, version moves 6 ms. The row adds no shadow or filter at rest (the selected edge is the inset shadow it already had), and each focus move adds one scroll write.
