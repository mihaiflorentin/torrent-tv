Under it is one chip line: a "Sort" label with the four sort chips, then a "Recent" label with the two most recent searches other than the term in the field, and Clear recent. ## Deviations and reasons

- **Search and Clear stay beside the field.** Typing only reads the local cache. The Search button is the one action that asks the trackers, and a remote cannot empty a field without Clear. Both are ordinary pills after the field, so the field still leads.
- **The selected sort chip uses the selected fill, a bold word and a saffron bar, not solid white.** This is the #53 rule: white means focus and nothing else.
- **Card lift is 1.045 and the ring is square-cornered on Chromium 53/63.** These are the #53 decisions, the same as on Home.
- **Cards keep the #55 copy (title on two lines and one fact) at rest and on focus.** The board's focused card shows "name · year" and its other cards show "year · resolution". The ticket asks for the shared cards. The #55 card already shows the name and a date fact, and it keeps one fixed height, so focusing a card changes no layout. This holds the D-pad cost down on a dense grid.
- **Lines are 24 px apart, not 36.** The #55 card copy is 128 px tall (two title lines and the fact), not the board's one 30 px meta line. Keeping the Library grid's 24 px fits a second line of cards on the first screen. The grid also starts 22 px lower than the board, because the chip line is 64 px (the TV target), not 56.
- **Two recent searches at most, each up to 260 px, then Clear recent.** The chip line is one focus row and must fit 1656 px without wrapping. The board shows two. The stored list still keeps eight. The term in the field is left out of the chips.
- **The count says "N results", not "N titles".** The number is the server's total for the query, not the loaded pages. It shows only while there is a query.

## Performance

Measured before and after this branch, alternating, three rounds each on this machine (`scratchpad/w58/perf.sh`). Each round runs the throttled lean harness (`node tools/tv_perf/run.mjs --lean`) and a grid probe. The probe opens Browse, Search results ("Kingdom") and the first Discover family, counts the compositor layers, then at 6x CPU throttle traces 5 Right, 3 Down, a held Down of 5 at 100 ms and 3 Up.

- **Harness.** After: three passes, no violations. The worst D-pad move per run was 39.9, 39.4 and 39.6 ms ("rails down" on Home), and the worst route change settled in 120 to 126 ms. Base: one run with a single violation (Left toward the menu from Library > Categories, 50.1 ms); worst moves 38.9 to 50.1 ms, route changes 125 to 134 ms.
- **Layers.** Browse and Search keep 7 layers before and after. The Discover family page drops from 9 to 7. The grid sits inside `.tv-content`, which is a paint-contained scroller, so cards do not get layers of their own, and no extra stacking-context rule was needed (`.poster-rail` has one since #55).
- **Grid moves.** Search results (8 cards, no paging): worst move 7 to 14 ms after, 13 to 16 ms before. Discover family (60 cards in 9 lines): worst 10.5 to 20.4 ms after, 16.7 to 23 ms before. Down now moves a line (8 to 9 ms a press) where before it did nothing. Browse is the dearest: its first move pages in the next 12 titles, which mounts a line and a half of cards and decodes their posters. Worst per run was 41.2, 43.3 and 34.3 ms, and the mean held Down was 17 to 18 ms. Before, Browse was a rail whose page came late, at the fourth card from its end. All of these are under the 50 ms budget.

## D-pad (maps `search`, `search-results`, `browse`, `catalog-family` and `library-family` updated)

- **Search.** Before a query it lands on the field; after a search it lands on the first result. The field row is input, Search and Clear. Down goes to the chip line in the nearest column, and Up there stays. The chip line is one row: Newest, Most seeded, Rating, A–Z (and Clear category when set), then the recent searches and Clear recent. Left from Newest opens the rail and Right at the end stays. Up goes to the field and Down to the grid's first line, both in the nearest column.
- **Grid (Search, Browse, family pages).** Left and Right move within a line. Left at column 0 opens the rail, and Right at a line's end stays. Up and Down keep the column, taking the nearest one on a shorter last line. Up from the first line goes to the chip line (Library family: the category chips). Down at the last line stays, or goes to Show more titles on a Discover family page, whose Up returns to the last line in the nearest column. Held Right from the first card stops at the seventh. Held Down walks one line a press, and the next page arrives two lines ahead of the cursor.
- **Back.** Back from a title page returns to its card. On a family page, Back closes the family onto its tile.

`node tools/tv_perf/dpad.mjs --only search,search-results,browse,catalog-family,categories,library-family,library-categories,tracker` passes (8 maps). The full audit also passes (see the PR).
