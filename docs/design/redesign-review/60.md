# #60 TV Settings in two panes, with Background jobs and Other projects: screenshot review

What changed: TV Settings is two panes. On the left the groups Playback, Trackers, Catalog, Server, Background jobs, Updates, Other projects (only while the server publishes project links) and Danger zone, each with a short status beside its name. On the right the selected group's rows, each with its value and a chevron, or a switch with On/Off. A row's help shows only while it has focus. Every control of the old single column is still there; the background-work limits moved into Background jobs, next to the entry to its screen. The Background jobs screen draws each job as a status icon and word, what ran ("Refresh · Copper Archive · FileList"), a relative time, then Details and Retry. A failed settings load, save, connection test, sync or update check is now one sentence instead of the raw error.

## How the captures were made

With the capture script, `node tools/tv_perf/screens.mjs tv` (headless Chromium at 1920x1080, Tizen 5 user agent, lean graphics, the synthetic household of `tools/tv_perf/fixture.mjs`). The journey gains two steps, `settings-trackers` (OK on Trackers) and `settings-server` (OK on Server, then Connection tests opened). "Before" is the integration tip f2b9250, built and captured with that tip's own journey; "after" is this branch. The board is `Settings.dc.html` from the mockup canvas at 1920x1080.

Committed here, downscaled to 720x405 so each stays under 300 KB: `60/before-settings.png`, `60/after-settings.png`, `60/after-settings-trackers.png`, `60/after-settings-server.png`, `60/before-jobs.png`, `60/after-jobs.png`. The full-size PNGs are 0.4 to 1 MB and stay out of git; `make screens-tv` regenerates them. Other projects and a job's detail look the same as before apart from the job detail heading, so they are not repeated here.

Point by point:

- **Layout and spacing:** the board's two columns at its own x positions (168 and 700) and widths (460 and 1124). The pane starts 14 px lower than the board's 180 px because the 56 px heading row of every screen is 96 px tall (#54); the gap is kept so Settings lines up with the other screens' content. The pane scrolls on its own, so the group list never moves.
- **Type:** group names 30 px semibold (bold when selected), statuses 26 px medium; the pane label 26 px semibold subtle with 0.02 em tracking; row names 30 px semibold, values 30 px regular muted with tabular figures; help 26 px at 1.4 line height. Nothing goes under the 26 px floor.
- **Colour and accent:** saffron only on an On switch and on Running jobs. "Needs attention" beside Trackers and Failed jobs use the danger colour; Completed jobs the ok colour. White is only focus.
- **Focus:** a focused group is the white pill with ground-coloured text (like the rail and tabs). A focused row keeps its own fill, lighter, inside the solid white ring (a field row draws it on the face behind its input) and shows its help. Rows do not lift; the help adds height only to the focused row, inside the pane.
- **Primary vs secondary:** Save and Fetch latest are the bolder names in their panes; Rebuild catalog is the danger word, filled with the danger colour on focus, alone in its group; Install server update and Rebuild still ask first in a dialog that opens on Cancel.

## D-pad (maps updated first, `clients/tv/src/dpad/maps/system.mjs`)

- `settings` (Playback, where Settings lands) owns the group list (`settings-group-<id>`, focus region `settings-groups`). Groups: Up and Down walk them and stop at both ends without changing the pane; OK or Right open the focused group on its first row; Left and Back open the rail, and Right from the rail returns to the group. Rows: Up and Down walk them and stop at both ends; Left (from the first column) and Back go to the selected group. Bursts: Down along the rows to Save and back up, Down along the groups from Playback to Danger zone.
- One map per pane: `settings-trackers`, `settings-catalog`, `settings-server`, `settings-diagnostics` (Server with the tests open; the tests keep their column four to a line), `settings-background-jobs`, `settings-updates`, `settings-other-projects`, `settings-danger-zone`. Each pane is told apart by `data-settings-pane`; the audit's screen signature now includes the open pane.
- `jobs` and `projects`: Back returns to `settings-jobs` in Background jobs and `settings-projects` in Other projects (the group opens with it). `job-detail` is unchanged (its #66 fixes hold: it opens on the back button, Back closes the detail first, the rail closes before the detail). `rebuild-dialog` returns to Rebuild catalog in the Danger zone; `setup` is reached through Server › Forget this server.
- The capture driver reaches Background jobs and Other projects through their groups (Left to the group list, Down to the group, OK, then the entry).

## Deviations, with reasons

- **Values are what is stored**: "en", "ro" rather than the board's "English", "Romanian". The rows are the text fields themselves (OK types into them), and the server stores language codes; naming the language would need a picker the TV does not have yet.
- **No "Pick subtitles automatically" switch**: the TV has no such setting (the subtitle mode is chosen per play in the Player). The board's switch row is used for the tracker switches instead.
- **Groups do not preview on focus**: the pane changes when a group is opened with OK or Right, not while Up and Down walk the list. Re-rendering the pane on every press would cost a render per held key, and the cursor should not land on a group whose rows it has not asked for. The focused group is the white pill, so the viewer always sees which one OK opens.
- **Background-work limits sit in Background jobs**, not in a separate group: the board has no "Background work" group, and the limits are about the jobs listed there.
- **Updates gained Check for updates**: the old screen had the action wired but no button; it keeps the Updates pane from being empty when no update notice shows.
- **The pane starts 14 px lower** (above).
- **Background jobs, Other projects and a job's detail have no board**: they take the Settings row vocabulary.
