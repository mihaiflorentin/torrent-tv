# #53 TV icon set, app mark, pill controls and the focus model: screenshot review

What changed: the shared TV vocabulary, on every screen at once. Line icons (one SVG module, `clients/tv/src/icons.tsx`) replace every Unicode glyph (rail, back buttons, row chevrons, the rating star); the app mark (saffron rounded square with a play shape) replaces the "TT" block in the rail and on Setup; buttons and chips are borderless translucent pills; and focus follows the new model: a focused button, chip, tab or rail item turns white with ground-coloured text, a focused row or poster keeps its own fill (lighter) inside the white ring, and posters, category tiles and the hero/title-page primary lift with a transform. Screen layouts, the rail's item list and every `data-focus-row`/`data-focus-col` are unchanged, so every screen still has its old arrangement: these are "new vocabulary, old layout" screens until the per-screen tickets (#54 to #62) restyle them.

## How the captures were made

The #65 capture script had not landed on the integration branch when this was done, so the captures use the research scripts (`shoot.mjs`, `shoot2.mjs`, plus a short `shoot53.mjs` that focuses a selected sort chip and a Delete button) against the `tools/tv_perf` fixture server, in headless Chromium at 1920x1080 with the Tizen 5 user agent (lean graphics). "Before" is the #52 after-set (the integration branch tip before this ticket, same scripts and fixture). The fixture has no versions, episodes or settings schema, so version rows, episode rows and the Settings fields are not in any capture; they are covered by the CSS focus-model test instead.

Committed here (under 300 KB each): `53/before-rail-open.png` / `53/after-rail-open.png` (rail open), `53/before-download-row.png` / `53/after-download-row.png` (a download card: saffron Play and red-bordered Delete before; pills and the calm danger focus after), `53/after-chips.png` (a selected sort chip under focus: white pill with the saffron marker), `53/after-title-actions.png` (title page actions with the lifted Resume), `53/after-info-panel.png`. The full-screen PNGs (300 to 840 KB) stay out of git; the maintainer can regenerate them with the #65 script.

Point by point:

- **Layout and spacing:** unchanged except one margin: the Home hero's two buttons now sit 16 px apart (without borders the pills touched). The rail icon column is re-centred on the 120 px strip (the 6 px accent bar it made room for is gone). Pills keep the 72 px target height and the 2 px (transparent) border, so nothing moves on focus.
- **Type:** buttons go to semibold 600 (labels and buttons per the type rules); primary stays bold.
- **Colour and accent:** saffron leaves every button fill and the rail bar. It remains on the app mark, progress bars, the selected marker under a selected chip/tab/toggle label (40 x 4 px), the selected episode's edge, the row chevrons and the card rating star.
- **Focus:** two signals everywhere, checked by `focus-ring.test.ts`: the 4 px white outline outside the 3 px offset (kept beside the halo for the webOS validator) plus a fill change (white pill on controls; `--tt-fill-selected` on rows; `--tt-overlay` under a poster's copy; `--tt-danger` on a destructive button). Rows and tabs never change geometry. Lift only by transform, eased over 120 ms on capable engines and snapped on `lean-graphics`.
- **Primary vs secondary:** primary is the brighter `--tt-fill-strong` pill with the bold word at rest and the white lifted pill under focus; secondaries are `--tt-fill` pills.

## Deviations and reasons

- **Selected chips and season tabs are not white at rest** (the boards draw the selected sort chip, filter chip and season pill white). White is the focus signal; a white selected chip next to a white focused one would leave the viewer guessing where the cursor is, which is the #1 complaint. Selected = selected fill + bold + saffron marker (the DESIGN rule "the selected-tab marker" in saffron); focused = white. Screen tickets can revisit per screen.
- **Rows use the outline, not the boards' `0 0 0 4px` box-shadow ring.** The webOS validator and the focus contract require a solid outline beside any box-shadow on `button:focus`; on Chromium 53/63 the outline is drawn as a rectangle around rounded rows and pills (rounded outlines need Chromium 94). The halo and the fill still follow the radius. Accepted: an obvious focus that never depends on one feature outranks the rounded corner.
- **Destructive buttons:** danger-coloured word on the ordinary pill, danger-filled pill when focused (no red border box). The boards hide Delete behind More; that move is #59.
- **Breadcrumb separators (›) stay as characters.** They are typography between words, like the middle dot, not icons; only the row chevrons became SVG.
- **The rating star** on a card is now a drawn star in saffron followed by the number (`cardRating`), with an aria-label "Rated 7.4"; the text line no longer carries "★".
- **Hero/title-page primary scale uses `--tt-focus-scale-tv` (1.045)**, not the boards' 1.06, to keep one lift value across posters and buttons.
- **Rail items stay 56 px with group labels**: the item list and its layout belong to #54.
