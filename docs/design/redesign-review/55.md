# #55 TV Home hero and denser cards: screenshot review

What changed: Home loses its hero panel and draws the hero straight on the focus-following backdrop under new left and bottom scrims. The board's type is used for the kind line, the title with its subtitle line, the facts with a resolution chip and a two-line overview. Resume names what is left, and Info and My List are round icon buttons in hero columns 1 and 2. Continue watching becomes landscape 16:9 cards with the progress bar on the art and the code with the time left under it. Every TV poster card is now 210 px wide (about seven across) and shows the poster, its library-state badge, the title on up to two lines and one short fact. `LibraryCard` is the one component for Library and Continue watching cards. Library family grids draw seven to a line. The backdrop mechanism (w780 picture decoded off the main thread, follow delay) is unchanged.

## Deviations and reasons

- **Blurred, not sharp, art behind the hero.** The board shows the title's sharp backdrop. Home keeps the focus-following w780 layer (blurred on capable engines, upscaled on lean ones), because the ticket keeps that mechanism unchanged and a full-resolution layer that follows focus would cost a decode per move. The scrims follow the board's shape, and the base scrim stays, so no part of the art is lighter than before.
- **No runtime in the hero facts.** `CatalogTitle` has none; the title page asks the Info endpoint for it, and Home does not add that request.
- **"S01E04", not "S01 · E04", on the landscape cards.** This is the code format every other TV surface and test uses.
- **Lift 1.045, not 1.06, and square rings on Chromium 53/63.** These are the #53 decisions (one lift value; the solid outline the webOS validator requires).
- **Card badges use short words on posters** ("Downloaded", "Partial", "41% done", "Failed", "Watched", "Started", with the full state in `aria-label`) and drop the dot. A 210 px poster does not fit "Some downloaded" at the 26 px floor. The fixture sets no library state, so the badges were checked in unit tests, not on screen.
- **Landscape titles wrap to two lines** (the board cuts at one with an ellipsis), so a long title is never cut mid-word ("Quiet Echo: The Ember Archive").
- **Home's first screen shows the Continue watching titles cut by the bottom edge.** The board is cut the same way.

## Performance

Throttled lean harness, `node tools/tv_perf/run.mjs --lean`, run alternately on the integration tip and this branch, three rounds each on this machine. The first version of the cards regressed Layerize from about 1.5 ms to 10–15 ms per move (hold Down 57–61 to 64–70 ms, menu moves 22 to 50 ms). The cause was in the compositor: a card is a positioned button, and inside a rail scroller that is not a stacking context every card near the viewport became its own layer (16 to 35 overlap layers on Home). With denser rows more cards were near the viewport. `performance.css` now makes `.poster-rail` a stacking context (`position: relative; z-index: 0`), so Home has 19 layers (before: 27). Final rounds, worst D-pad move per run: base 40.8, 45.3, 53.1 ms (one over budget); after 46.7, 42.9, 44.5 ms. Mean hold-Down move: base 17.9–21.8 ms, after 17.7–22.8 ms. Route changes: after 128–147 ms, all within 150 ms; base failed once on Downloads (164.8 ms).

## D-pad (maps `home`, `home-info`, and `library-family` updated)

- **Home.** It lands on the hero primary. The hero row is col 0 (primary), col 1 (Info, the existing column) and col 2 (My List, appended). Left and Right walk it: Left from the primary opens the rail, Right at My List stays, Up stays, Down enters Continue watching in the same column, and Back opens the rail.
- **Rows.** Up and Down keep the column (Up from a card past column 2 returns to My List). Left from column 0 opens the rail, and Right at the end stays.
- **Info panel (`home-info`).** OK on Info opens it, and Back or Close returns to Info.
- **My List.** OK toggles it in place.
- **Library family grid.** It is seven to a line now; the burst from the first card stops at the seventh.
