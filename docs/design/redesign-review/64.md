# #64 Browser rail grouped into the six hubs: screenshot review

What changed: the browser rail holds the TV's six hubs (Home, Search, Library, Discover, Downloads, Settings) instead of fifteen items in four groups (Home and Search; My Library; Tracker; Jobs, Projects, Bug Report and Settings). Library and Discover draw their sections as tabs on the heading's line; Background jobs, Other projects and Report a bug moved to the end of the Settings heading line, and Background jobs and Other projects lead back with a "Settings" pill. Every URL is unchanged and lands on its hub and tab (`web/hubs.ts`, grouped exactly as `clients/tv/src/hubs.ts`). The desktop shell (the server control app in `desktop/`) has its own five-section rail and is not affected.

## How the captures were made

`make screens-web` (`node tools/tv_perf/screens.mjs web`: the built browser app against the synthetic household at 1440x900 and 390x844, all 21 steps). "Before" is the integration tip 89761fd (#52, #65, #53, #63, #66, #61, #56, #54), built and captured with the same script; "after" is this branch. The 641 to 1099 px icon rail and the 320 px phone were checked with the same journey at 900x700 and 320x700 (a throwaway wrapper that swaps the script's viewport list). The boards are the TV's 1920x1080 canvas; the nearest is "Navigation open" (2) for the rail and Search (5) for the chip strip the tabs reuse.

Committed here (desktop downscaled to 720 px wide, phone at 390 px, each under 180 KB): `64/{before,after}-desktop-continue.png`, `64/{before,after}-desktop-settings.png`, `64/{before,after}-desktop-jobs.png`, `64/{before,after}-phone-discover.png`, `64/{before,after}-phone-settings.png`. The full set stays out of git; `make screens-web` regenerates it under `tools/tv_perf/.out/screens/web/`.

Point by point:

- **Layout and spacing:** the rail keeps its widths (232, 72 px, bottom bar 64 px) and the 44 px item height; items are 4 px apart with no group gaps or hairlines. Tabs sit on the heading's line, 32 px after it and 8 px apart, and wrap under it when they do not fit (phones). The content starts higher on Library tabs (the duplicated shelf heading is gone).
- **Type:** rail labels unchanged (15 px medium, bold when current); the phone bar keeps the 13 px floor with -0.01 em tracking so "Downloads" fits. Headings unchanged in size.
- **Colour and accent:** saffron only on the selected bar (rail item, tab) and the app mark; Settings pills are the ordinary `--tt-fill` pills.
- **Focus:** native buttons in the Tab order; a focused rail item, tab or Settings pill is white inside the ring (#63); hover is one fill step. The selected tab under focus turns white and its bar darkens, as on season tabs.
- **Primary vs secondary:** the rail and tabs are navigation; the Settings pills are secondary (no primary on the heading line).

## Deviations, with reasons

- **No hint under the hovered or focused rail item, no Downloads count, no connection foot.** On the TV the open rail is an overlay that grows the focused item; the browser rail is always open beside the page, and growing an item on hover would shift the list under the pointer. The browser shows connection problems in its own dialogs and Downloads shows its counts on the page.
- **Tabs push history entries.** A tab is a view with its own URL (unchanged from before), so browser Back steps back through tabs, as it stepped back through the old rail items.
- **Home and Search keep their `h1`.** The TV draws no heading there; the browser keeps one heading per page for screen readers and the page title, at the existing size.
- **Report a bug stays.** The TV has no bug report; the browser keeps it, now as a Settings pill instead of a rail item.
- **Other projects only while the server publishes links** (as on the TV); a shared `/projects` link still opens the page with its empty state.
- **Settings entries are pills on the heading line, not rows in the page.** The browser's Settings form is long (fields, then the update section); a section at its end was out of sight below the form (tried, captured, moved).
